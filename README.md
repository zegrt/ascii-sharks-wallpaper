## Get it in a minute

1. **[Download ASCII Sharks](https://github.com/zegrt/ascii-sharks-wallpaper/releases/latest/download/ASCII-Sharks.dmg)**, open it, and drag the app into **Applications**.
2. **Open it.** macOS will stop it the first time, because it isn't from a registered Apple developer. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**. You only do this once.
3. **That's it.** Sharks are swimming on your desktop, and a little 🐟 sits in your menu bar.

## Things to try

- 🖱 **Move your mouse** over the desktop. The fish scatter and leave a ripple behind.
- 💦 **Click empty desktop** for a splash.
- 🦈 **Choose your sharks.** Up to eight, from eight species: hammerhead, tiger, mako, thresher and more.
- 🌗 **Dark, Light or Calm.** Calm is dimmer, so your desktop icons stand out.
- 🖥 **Two screens?** They join into one ocean, and sharks swim from one to the other.
- ⏸ **⌃⌥⌘P** pauses it from anywhere.

It's all in the 🐟 menu and in **Settings**.

## Good to know

- **Easy on your battery.** It stops when the desktop is covered or your Mac is locked or asleep. It slows down on battery and holds still in Low Power Mode.
- **Your lock screen matches.** While it runs, a still of the ocean becomes your wallpaper too. Quit, and your own wallpaper comes back.
- **It keeps itself up to date.** New versions install themselves in a second or two. Rather choose when? Turn that off in Settings.
- **Private.** No accounts, no ads, no tracking. The only thing it ever asks the internet is "is there a new version?"
- **Easy to remove.** 🐟 menu → Help → **Uninstall ASCII Sharks…** puts your wallpaper back and deletes everything it saved.

<details>
<summary><b>Privacy, in detail</b></summary>

- **The one network request:** about once a day, the app asks GitHub for the latest version of this project. It sends the app's version and nothing else. When there's an update, it downloads it from GitHub. You can turn checking off in Settings.
- **The pointer and windows:** it notices where your pointer is and where windows sit, only so the fish can react and so it can pause when the desktop is covered. It can't see what you type or what's inside your windows, and it needs no special permissions.
- **Problem reports:** logs stay on your Mac (`~/Library/Logs/ASCII Sharks`). **Report a Problem** opens a draft on GitHub with your name and folders removed, and nothing is sent until you submit it.

</details>
